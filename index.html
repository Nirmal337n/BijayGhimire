About Me

9+ Years of Experience

I am Bijaya Ghimire, a passionate professional chef from Banepa-02, Kavre, Nepal. With more than 9 years of experience in Indian and international cuisine, I bring creativity and precision to every plate.

Currently working as Chef de Partie, I specialise in food presentation, taste balance, and kitchen leadership — preparing hot dishes, salads, sandwiches, pasta, and local & international cuisine.

I've honed my craft at Mandala Cafe and Restaurant and Hotel Mystic Mountain, with a strong emphasis on hygiene, clean workstations, and food safety.

  • Nepalese chef with experience in 2 professional kitchens
  • Comfortable in hot kitchen, cold section, breakfast & à la carte
  • Strong focus on food hygiene and safe working practices
  • Reliable and able to handle busy service hours
  • Currently available for opportunities in Nepal or abroad
